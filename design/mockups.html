Pilcrow

The look of the Markdown editor: a logo, three screens, light and dark. Pilcrow is a proposed name; the pilcrow (¶) is the proofreader's sign for "show me the marks", which is what this app's main button does.

Logo

A pilcrow whose second stroke is the text cursor. The same shape is the button that switches between the formatted view and the Markdown source.

Pilcrow

Left to right: the app icon, the icon at home-screen sizes, the one-colour versions for Android's themed icons, and the name next to the mark.

Light

Markdown marks are always the blue pencil. The cursor is always the red pencil, and so is anything that is not saved yet. Nothing else has colour.

The same screens in the colours of your Obsidian theme: cream surface, coral for the marks and the cursor.

Dark

True black, because the phone has an OLED screen: black pixels are switched off.

The warm dark surface of your Obsidian theme.

Colour and type

One type family in two widths. Switching to Source changes the spacing of the letters, not the letters, so the note still looks like the same note.
